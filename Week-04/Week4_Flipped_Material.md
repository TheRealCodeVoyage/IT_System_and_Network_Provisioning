## Introduction to Packer

Terraform provisions infrastructure; Packer builds the images that infrastructure runs. Before we get into the "why," watch the introduction to the tool itself.

From **"Automating Image Creation with HashiCorp Packer for DevOps"** by Bryan Krausen, watch:
- **Watch:** Chapter 2 — Introduction to HashiCorp Packer [Automating Image Creation with HashiCorp Packer for DevOps](https://learning.oreilly.com/videos/automating-image-creation/9781805128175/9781805128175-video2_1/)
