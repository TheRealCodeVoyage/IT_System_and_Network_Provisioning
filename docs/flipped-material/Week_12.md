## Getting Ready for Week 13 and the Final

Week 13 covers Test 1, Test 2, and Test 3 review, plus general final-exam prep. The final is comprehensive — it's the only assessment that covers **everything**, including two topics no test touched directly: Week 11's PXE demo and this week's Terraform state/remote backend material.

Before Week 13, revisit:

- **Weeks 1-3 (Test 1 territory):** dev environment tooling, SSH/Bash/systemd, the AWS CLI and IAM basics.
- **Weeks 4-6 (Test 2 territory):** Terraform fundamentals, Packer + Cloud-Init, and Ansible's basics (inventory, ad-hoc commands, playbooks).
- **Week 7 discussion + Weeks 9-10 (Test 3 territory):** physical vs. virtual vs. containerized infrastructure, Terraform variables/modules, and Ansible's loops/conditionals/handlers/roles.
- **Week 11 and today (final-exam-only territory):** the PXE boot chain (DHCP, TFTP, HTTP) and Terraform state/remote backends — since no test covered these, they're fair game on the final without a prior test as a checkpoint.

Come to Week 13 with specific questions — from old tests, labs, or anything that's still fuzzy. That session runs best as a review driven by what the class actually needs revisited, not a re-lecture of everything.
