## Ansible: Flow Control, Roles & Collections

Week 6 covered Ansible's basics — inventory, ad-hoc commands, and simple playbooks. Week 10 puts everything together in one full session: loops, conditionals, handlers, and roles. This reading gets you ready for that.

From **"Red Hat Certified Engineer (RHCE) Ansible Automation Study Guide"**, read:
- **Read:** Chapter 5 — Flow Control (loops, conditionals, and handlers in a playbook)
- **Read:** Chapter 8 — Ansible Roles and Collections, specifically the *Ansible Roles* and *Developing a Role* sections

As you read, notice the parallel to today's lecture: an Ansible role is organized the same way a Terraform module is — a self-contained, reusable directory with a predictable internal structure that something else can call with different inputs.
